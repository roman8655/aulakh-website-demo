Assessment Services

Autism Assessments

A respectful, unhurried path toward understanding yourself.

We offer respectful, neurodiversity-affirming autism assessments for adolescents and adults in the Halifax area. Because autism assessments are detailed and time-intensive, we begin with an intake appointment to make sure a full assessment is genuinely the right next step for you — and if it isn't, we'll help you find a path that fits better.

"You don't need to change who you are to be understood."
— Our Approach to Autism Assessments

Do You Think You May Be Autistic?

Understanding yourself is a meaningful place to start
You might relate to
  • Experiencing the world through different sensory intensities — sounds, textures, lights, or sensations that others don't seem to notice.
  • Preferring routine, predictability, or deep focus on specific interests.
  • Finding some social situations effortful, confusing, or exhausting, even when you care about the people involved.
  • Communicating in ways that feel natural to you, but that others sometimes misread.

Maybe you've always sensed you experience the world a little differently — without always knowing why, or having the language to describe it.

An assessment isn't about fitting a mould. It's about gaining a clearer, more compassionate understanding of how you're wired — your communication style, sensory experiences, social differences, and individual strengths.

What the Assessment Involves

A comprehensive, multi-method approach
We Gather Information in Several Ways

To ensure accuracy and a meaningful result, a full autism assessment doesn't rely on any single measure or conversation. It draws on:

  • A structured diagnostic interview
  • A formal autism assessment measure, such as the ADOS
  • Review of previous records or assessments
  • Input from people who know you well — a parent, partner, or other trusted person
Why It Matters

Every individual's experiences and needs are unique, so the time required can vary depending on the complexity of your concerns and the information available.

We provide clear timelines and expectations from the outset, while remaining flexible — adapting the process to best reflect your goals and circumstances.

Please Note

A full ASD assessment is a significant investment of time and cost. That's why we always begin with an intake session — together, we'll determine whether moving forward is genuinely the right fit for your needs. If it isn't, your clinician will discuss other options that may serve you better, such as a different type of assessment or therapeutic support.

The Assessment Process

A thoughtful, unhurried path toward clarity
Step 1

Getting Started

Reach out to get started — no referral is required. We'll ask a few simple questions, like who referred you and whether supporting information might be available (for example, from parents, partners, school records, or previous assessments). Once your intake is scheduled, you'll receive online rating scales and a background questionnaire to complete beforehand.

Step 2 · Approx. 2 Hours

Intake Session

An in-depth conversation about your concerns, history, and what you're hoping to gain from an assessment. Feel free to send or bring any previous report cards or assessments. Together with your clinician, you'll decide whether a full assessment is the right next step — if you move forward, timelines and costs will be clearly explained.

Step 3 · Approx. 2-3 Hours

Full Assessment

A structured diagnostic interview and a formal autism assessment measure, such as the ADOS — this may be split across two sessions if needed. We also gather input from people who know you well, and review relevant records, shared in writing, by phone, or by video, whatever feels most comfortable.

Step 4 · Approx. 3 Hours + 1 Hour

Report & Feedback

Your clinician scores and interprets all the information gathered, then prepares a detailed diagnostic summary letter with personalized recommendations. You'll return for a feedback session to discuss your results and next steps together.

Every individual's experiences and needs are unique, so the time required may vary depending on the complexity of your concerns and the information available. We provide clear timelines and expectations from the outset, while remaining flexible to your goals and circumstances.
