Who We Are

We are a team of experienced Registered Psychologists and Registered Clinical Social Workers who provide compassionate, collaborative, and personalized care, grounded in current best practices.

Our clinicians work with clients across a wide range of ages and developmental stages, including children, adolescents, young adults, adults, parents, and couples. We also have specialized experience supporting individuals who work in, or have previously worked in, high-risk environments such as veterans, first responders, and corrections officers.

Our team has extensive experience addressing a broad range of mental health concerns and life stressors, including anxiety, obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), stress, depression, adjustment to major life changes, trauma, grief and loss, relationship challenges, workplace stress and burnout, as well as chronic disabilities and illnesses.

We are known for our warm, empathic, and collaborative approach. Using evidence-based treatments, we work alongside our clients to better understand their difficulties, develop effective coping strategies, manage identified challenges, and strengthen their unique personal resources and abilities.

It is both our privilege and pleasure to support our clients as they move forward in meaningful and positive ways in their lives.

Theraputic Services

Therapeutic approaches tailored to meet your individual needs.

Our therapy services are designed to support the whole person, not just the challenges you may be facing. We take the time to understand your experiences, strengths, relationships, and goals, working collaboratively to build a space where you feel heard, respected, and supported.

Together, we focus on developing insight, strengthening coping strategies, and creating meaningful, sustainable change. Whether you are navigating a specific concern or seeking personal growth, therapy can offer a space for reflection, skill-building, and moving forward in a way that aligns with what matters most to you.

Assessment Services

Clarity, Insight, and a Path Forward.

Our psychological assessments are designed to provide a comprehensive, whole-person understanding of each individual. We look beyond symptoms to explore strengths, skills, supports, and challenges—helping to build a clear picture of what's needed to support well-being, navigate difficulties, and move toward meaningful goals.

Whether you are seeking answers, validation, or direction, our assessments are intended to offer clarity, insight, and practical recommendations you can use in everyday life.

ADHD Assessments

Our ADHD assessments provide a thoughtful and comprehensive understanding of attention, executive functioning, and related challenges. Assessments can help clarify strengths, identify areas of difficulty, and guide personalized recommendations and supports.

Autism Assessments

Dr. Kate Robertson, PhD

Trauma. Psychodynamic Therapy. Attachment-Based Therapy.

Dr. Kate Robertson, PhD (she/her/hers)

Practice Leader, Registered Psychologist

Dr. Robertson is a clinical psychologist, holding registration with the Nova Scotia Board of Examiners of Psychologists. She received her doctorate in Clinical Psychology from the Graduate Center at City University of New York. She has wide-ranging training and clinical experience working with children, youth, and adults who struggle with mild to severe mental health difficulties. She has worked in a variety of settings, including adolescent and adult inpatient units, community mental health services, and university counselling centers. Prior to working full time at the practice, Dr. Robertson worked at the Garron Centre, the Child and Adolescent Mental Health Unit at IWK Health for more than 6 years. Dr. Robertson helps foster change with her clients who struggle with a variety of life's stresses, including depression, anxiety, trauma, and relationship concerns. She has extensive experience working with individuals who wish to repair and foster connections with themselves and those close to them.

Dr. Robertson is a firm believer in the power of the therapeutic relationship and strives to create a safe, healthy, and authentic space for clients to begin their journey of psychological growth and change. She believes that effective therapy is a co-created process where she and the client work together as a team. Acknowledging and understanding that change can be difficult and hard work, she meets each client with warmth and compassion. Dr. Robertson's style combines approaches that work to increase an individual's and family's self-knowledge and insight (key components of the psychodynamic psychotherapy, EMDR, and attachment-based therapy) with practical techniques like coping strategies, skills building and goal-setting (cognitive behaviour therapy and dialectical behaviour therapy). She uses attachment and trauma lenses to better understand life's stressors and is trained in Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR). Above all, Dr. Robertson is adaptable to meet each client's individual needs and engages with each person with curiosity and empathy.
