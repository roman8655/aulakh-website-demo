Mental Health Assessments

A thoughtful, whole-person path toward clarity.

Seeking a diagnostic mental health assessment is an important step toward better understanding yourself. We offer comprehensive, in-person assessments for adolescents and adults in the Halifax area, approaching every concern with care, curiosity, and attention to the whole person — not just a single set of symptoms.

"The clearest path forward starts with understanding where you truly are."
— Our Approach to Mental Health Assessments

A Whole-Person Approach

Not just a single set of symptoms
We Look at the Whole Picture

A thorough diagnostic assessment considers how different experiences interact and influence one another. In addition to your primary concerns, we take time to understand:

  • Anxiety and OCD
  • Mood changes
  • Physical health
  • Life stressors
  • Substance use
  • Family dynamics and early upbringing
Why It Matters

By exploring these interconnected areas, we develop a more accurate and meaningful understanding of your concerns — helping to ensure that any diagnosis, insights, and recommendations truly reflect your unique experiences and needs.

Please Note

If there are concerns about a specific learning disorder — such as challenges with reading, writing, or math — a psychoeducational assessment may be recommended instead. This helps us accurately differentiate between learning differences and attention-related concerns like ADHD.

The Assessment Process

Clear steps, guided with care
Step 1

Getting Started

Once your assessment is booked, you'll receive online rating scales and a background questionnaire to help us learn more about your history. You're also encouraged to share any helpful documents in advance — previous assessments, report cards or academic records, workplace performance reviews, or documentation of past supports or accommodations.

Step 2 · 3 Hours

Assessment Session

You'll meet with a clinician in person for a comprehensive assessment session, including a clinical interview, standardized rating scales, and a review of relevant records.

Step 3 · 3 Hours

Scoring & Report Writing

Your clinician scores all measures, integrates the information gathered, and prepares a detailed diagnostic summary letter with personalized recommendations. When helpful, collateral input from a partner, parent, or other trusted person can also be included — shared in writing or through a brief phone or video meeting. This is optional, but can offer valuable perspective.

Step 4 · 1 Hour

Feedback Session

You'll return for a feedback session where we review your results and next steps together.

Please note that additional time may be required in more complex situations, or if further consultation is needed.
